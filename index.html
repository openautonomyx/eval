About SFIA Evaluator

How It Works

SFIA Evaluator connects to your public social accounts and analyzes your publicly available content to assess your skills against the SFIA (Skills Framework for the Information Age) framework.

  1. Connect your GitHub, Medium, or other social accounts
  2. We scrape publicly available content (repositories, articles, projects)
  3. Our AI analyzes the content against SFIA skill definitions
  4. You receive a proficiency level (1-7) for each relevant skill
  5. All sources are documented for full transparency

SFIA Framework

The Skills Framework for the Information Age (SFIA) is a global skills and competency framework for the digital world. It provides a common language for describing skills and competencies across 7 levels of responsibility.
